ZFIGHT

CC27 3rd Place Winner

Made with TI-Basic!

Controls:
[2nd] : Ok
[Alpha] : Back
[Mode] : Pause
[Arrows]: Move

Story:

The world is made out of triangles.
Everything is made out of triangles.
But for a triangle to exists, there must be a first.
And having that first triangle means great control.
And so, if someone was to take that first triangle, they can cause chaos in the world.
Unless you stop them.

25 others have sacrificed their lives, you are their last hope
Thats why they call this fight
the
ZFIGHT

The group file is given, but if that doesn't transfer, try each individual file

Required Files:
ZFIGHT - Program
ZFIGHTDT - Tilemap
ZFIGHTST - Tilemap Sprites
ZFIGHTSS - Sprites

ZFIGHTSV - Your Save {Stored in RAM}
